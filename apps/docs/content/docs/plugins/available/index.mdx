---
title: Available Plugins
description: Guides to Paradev plugins and the services they connect to
---

Install these from **Plugins** in the sidebar. Each one adds MCP tools to [supported agents](/plugins/overview#supported-agents) and skills that teach the agent when to use them.

| Plugin | Category | Connects to |
|:-------|:---------|:------------|
| [GitHub](/plugins/available/github) | Developer tools | Your GitHub account |
| [Sentry](/plugins/available/sentry) | Developer tools | Your Sentry organization |
| [Resend](/plugins/available/resend) | Developer tools | Your Resend team |
| [Stripe](/plugins/available/stripe) | Data & APIs | Your Stripe account |
| [Peec AI](/plugins/available/peec) | Data & APIs | Your Peec AI account |
| [Linear](/plugins/available/linear) | Productivity | Your Linear workspace |
| [Notion](/plugins/available/notion) | Productivity | Your Notion workspace |
| [Granola](/plugins/available/granola) | Productivity | Your Granola account |
| [Circleback](/plugins/available/circleback) | Productivity | Your Circleback account |
| [YNAB](/plugins/available/ynab) | Productivity | Your YNAB account |
| [Slack](/plugins/available/slack) | Communication | Your Slack workspace |
| [Gmail](/plugins/available/gmail) | Communication | Your Google account |
| [Superhuman](/plugins/available/superhuman) | Communication | Your Superhuman account |

The built-in Paradev skills are not a plugin you install. They come with the app; see [Skills](/skills).
