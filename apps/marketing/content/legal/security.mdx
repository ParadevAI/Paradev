## Reporting a security vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in Paradev (the desktop app, web app, mobile app, CLI, or any service we operate), email **security@paradev.com**. Include enough detail to reproduce the issue. We respond within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

Please do not open public issues for security reports.

Our compliance posture, certifications, and security documentation live in the [Paradev Trust Center](https://trust.superset.sh).

## Reporting abusive content

If you encounter phishing, malware, or other abusive content, report it to **security@paradev.com**. We review abuse reports promptly and take down content that violates our [Terms of Service](/terms).
