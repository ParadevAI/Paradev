## 1. Introduction

Superset Inc. ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our desktop or mobile applications.

Please read this policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access our services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Data:** When you sign up for our waitlist or create an account (including via Sign in with Apple, GitHub, or Google), we may collect your name, email address, and other contact information.
- **Connected Accounts:** If you connect a third-party account (such as Google, GitHub, Linear, Notion, Sentry, or Slack), we store the access credentials for that account and the data the connected service returns for the features you use. Section 5 describes Google data in full.
- **User Content:** Messages, tasks, and attachments (such as images) you send to AI agents through our applications are transmitted to and stored by our services so they can be delivered to your workspaces and synced across your devices.
- **Communications:** When you contact us for support or feedback, we collect the information you provide in those communications.
- **Payment Data:** If you make a purchase, payment information is processed by our third-party payment processors. We do not store your full payment card details.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data:** We collect information about how you interact with our services, including pages visited, screens viewed, features used, and time spent. We use analytics providers (such as PostHog) to process this data, associated with your account identifier, name, and email address.
- **Device Data:** We may collect device identifiers, operating system version, browser type, app version, and similar technical information.
- **Log Data:** Our servers automatically record information such as IP address, access times, and referring URLs.

### 2.3 Desktop Application

When you work on your own machine, our desktop application runs locally and we do not have access to your source code, terminal commands, or file contents unless you explicitly choose to share diagnostic information with us. Section 2.4 describes cloud workspaces, which run on machines we host.

### 2.4 Cloud Workspaces

Cloud workspaces run on machines that we host for you through our hosting provider. While a cloud workspace or environment exists on our platform, we store the data on it, including the code you check out there and saved copies of its disk. To operate cloud workspaces, we collect operational logs from our own services on each machine (such as the boot steps, the memory guard, the remote desktop session, and our host service) and from our API.

### 2.5 Mobile Application

Our mobile application lets you monitor and chat with AI agents running in your workspaces. With your permission, it can access your photo library, camera, and microphone so you can attach images to messages and dictate text; speech recognition may be processed by Apple. Photos and dictated text are only transmitted when you choose to send them. The mobile application collects the same account, usage, and device data described above.

## 5. Google User Data

This section describes how Paradev accesses, uses, stores, and shares data from your Google Account. It adds to the rest of this policy, and it controls if the two ever disagree.

### 5.1 Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile picture. We use them to create your Paradev account, to sign you in, and to show you to your teammates. Signing in with Google gives us no access to your Gmail, Calendar, Drive, or any other Google service.

### 5.2 Optional Google Plugins and Automations

Paradev's Gmail and Google Calendar plugins are optional. Creating a Paradev account or signing in with Google does not authorize these plugins to access your mail or calendar. To use them, you connect a Google Account and approve access on Google's authorization screen. Access is limited to the connected account and the permissions you grant.

The Gmail plugin lets an AI agent read, search, organize, draft, send, and delete mail at your request. The Google Calendar plugin lets an agent read calendars, check availability, and create, update, or delete events at your request. Both plugins use Paradev's shared Google connection, whose authorization screen requests Gmail and Calendar permissions together.

You can also connect Google to use Gmail automations. The Gmail automation connection flow requests read-only mail access. When a Google connection grants Gmail read access, Paradev subscribes to new-mail notifications and records incoming-message metadata in the background to support automations. This can happen before you configure an automation or when no automation matches a message. Your configured filters determine which messages start automation runs; a new chat request is not required for each message.

The permissions below support these optional features. The permissions requested depend on the connection flow; Google's authorization screen shows the access you are being asked to grant. We use each permission only for the purpose listed:

- `openid`, `email`: identify which Google Account you connected, and show its address in Paradev.
- `gmail.readonly`: read message headers and labels, so an automation can start when new mail arrives, and read the messages and attachments you ask an agent to open.
- `gmail.compose`, `gmail.modify`: create and send drafts, and change labels or read state, when you ask an agent to.
- `gmail.labels`: create, rename, and delete Gmail labels at your request.
- `gmail.settings.basic`: read and manage the Gmail filters you ask an agent to set up.
- `https://mail.google.com/`: Google grants some mailbox operations only under full mail access, including permanently deleting a message or a thread.
- `calendar.readonly`: list your calendars, read event details, and check availability when you ask an agent to.
- `calendar.events`: create, update, and delete calendar events when you ask an agent to.

### 5.3 Google Data We Store

- **Credentials:** The access token and refresh token for the connected account. We encrypt them before we write them to our database and use them to maintain the connection and call the Google APIs listed in Section 5.2.
- **Account details:** The connected mailbox address, your Google account identifier, your Google profile name, and the scopes you granted, so we can show you which account is connected and keep the connection working.
- **Mail metadata for automations:** For incoming messages processed by mailbox monitoring, we record the subject, sender, To and Cc addresses, date, message and thread identifiers, Gmail labels, and whether the message has an attachment. We record this metadata before checking whether an automation matches.
- **Plugin results and agent history:** When an agent uses a Google plugin, retrieved message content, attachments, or calendar details can become part of tool results, conversations, agent session files, or files the agent saves. Depending on where the agent runs and the features you use, these records can reside on your machine or in our hosted workspaces and cloud chat history.

Mailbox monitoring stores the metadata described above, not message bodies or attachment contents. This is separate from plugin use: content retrieved by an agent can remain in its history or saved files even after you disconnect Google.

### 5.4 How We Use and Share Google Data

- We use Google data only to run the features you turn on. We do not use it for any other purpose.
- When you use the Gmail or Google Calendar plugin with an AI agent, the agent sends the mail, attachments, or calendar details it retrieves to its AI provider to carry out your request and returns the results in that conversation. Section 4 and our [Subprocessors](/subprocessors) page describe the service providers we engage.
- If you use an agent through your own provider account or a provider endpoint you configure, that provider also processes the data under your agreement and settings with it. Review that provider's retention and model-training controls before connecting Google data to the agent. Our Limited Use commitments below continue to apply to Paradev's handling of Google data.
- Mail from a connected account is scoped to the person who connected it. Only that member's automations can match its messages.
- No one at Paradev reads your Google data unless you explicitly agree to our viewing specific data for support, access is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or the law requires it.

### 5.5 Limited Use

Paradev's use of data received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements. We do not:

- Use Google user data for advertising of any kind, including personalized, targeted, or retargeted advertising.
- Sell or transfer Google user data to data brokers, information resellers, or any other party that resells data.
- Use Google user data for credit assessment, lending, or any similar decision.
- Use Google user data to train or improve generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
- Transfer Google user data to anyone except with your consent to provide or improve the features you use, as necessary for security or legal compliance, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets after obtaining your explicit prior consent.

### 5.6 Retention and Deletion of Google Data

- You can disconnect Google in Paradev's connection settings. Disconnecting disables future access through that connection. It does not necessarily revoke the grant at Google or erase stored credentials: a disconnected plugin connection can retain encrypted tokens until its connection record is deleted. Other active connections to the same Google account can continue to operate.
- You can revoke Paradev's grant directly in your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revocation prevents further authorized access under that grant. Google may still deliver notifications already in transit.
- Disconnecting or revoking access does not erase Google data already included in conversations, agent session files, saved files, or automation records. Remove local copies on the machine where the agent runs. Contact [privacy@paradev.com](mailto:privacy@paradev.com) to request deletion of hosted copies and other Google data held by Paradev.
- Mail event metadata supports filter evaluation, delivery retries, and automation history. Event intake records undergo scheduled cleanup; copies included in conversations or automation runs follow the retention of those records. Account deletion does not automatically erase all Google data in a shared organization's records. Sections 6 and 8 describe our general retention and account-deletion practices.
