## Terminal Tab Arrangement <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/980" />

- Drag and drop tabs to reorder them and keep a workspace organized
- Tooltips show keyboard navigation hints for adjacent tabs
- A new workspace gets a branch name generated from its title

## Docs and Blog Support <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/974" />

- The docs sidebar has a simpler structure of sections and items
- The logo in the navigation bar links to the marketing URL
- Environment variables load with dotenv during development
- Overscroll no longer triggers pull-to-refresh
- The marketing redirect now reads the docs URL from an environment variable

## Ghostty Terminal Support <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/950" />

Ghostty is now in the list of applications under "Open In" in the desktop app. Pick it to open files in your preferred terminal.
