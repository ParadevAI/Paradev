## The Right Pane for Everyone <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/9008" />

The right pane is now on for everyone. Keep Changes, Files, Pages, a browser or a terminal open beside your agent, each in its own tab.

- **+** opens a New Tab screen with every tool and its hotkey, this workspace's newest pages, and its pull requests <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8965" />
- Files open as tabs with preview and pinned states, as in an editor <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/8964" />
- The main **+** menu has an **Open in right pane** section <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/9019" />
- Agents can open and arrange it too, with `superset panes` <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/9204" />

```bash
superset panes open changes
superset panes open file src/app.ts --line 42
superset panes sidebar show --full-screen
```

Open it with ⌘⇧G for Changes or ⌘⇧B for a browser, or click **+** in the right pane.

## A New Workspaces List <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/9115" />

The **Workspaces** page now works like an issue tracker. Pick a built-in view (**All**, **Needs me**, **Working**), add filter chips for host, project, creator or status, and save the result as your own view.

- **Display** switches between list and board, groups by status, project, host or creator, and orders by the last agent message, creation time or name
- Long lists scroll smoothly, and your choices are kept

Open **Workspaces** in the sidebar.

## Group Your Automations <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/9210" />

Automations can now live in groups. Right-click an automation and choose **Move to group**, or type a new name to create one.

- Drag a row, or a whole selection, onto another group
- ⌘-click to select several, then move, pause, resume or delete them at once
- From the CLI: `superset automations update <id> --group "Daily check-ins"` <PRBadge url="https://github.com/superset-sh/superset/pull/9209" />
