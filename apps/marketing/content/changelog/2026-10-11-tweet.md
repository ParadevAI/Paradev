Stop tabbing to GitHub to check your PR. @superset_sh now gives every pull request its own page: diffs, checks, reviews, and a comment box that posts as you, right in the workspace.

Plus a bunch of bug fixes. Thanks to everyone in the v2 open beta for the reports, please keep them coming!
