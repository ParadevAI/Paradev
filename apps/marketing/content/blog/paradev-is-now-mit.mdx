As of November 4, 2026, Paradev is open source under the [MIT License](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/main/LICENSE.md). Before that it was under the Elastic License 2.0, which let you read the code but limited how you could use it.

Now you can read it, fork it, change it, and ship it, commercially too. The [TypeScript SDK](https://docs.paradev.com/sdk/getting-started) moved from Apache-2.0 to MIT as well, so the whole repo is under one license.

## Run it on your own cloud

You also choose where your agents run.

- **On your machines:** your laptop, a Mac mini under the desk, or any Linux server in AWS, GCP, Hetzner, or wherever you already are. Your code and agent sign-ins never leave it. [Here's how](https://docs.paradev.com/bring-your-own-cloud).
- **In our cloud:** [cloud workspaces](https://docs.paradev.com/cloud-workspaces) keep your agents going while your laptop is closed.

Either way, you check on them from desktop, iPhone, or iPad.

## What stays the same

Your account, plan, workspaces, and settings. Agents still use your own subscriptions and keys, and Paradev never proxies model calls.

## Come build with us

The code is at [github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev). Issues and pull requests are welcome, and [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) has the setup.
