As of November 4, 2026, Paradev is open source under the [MIT License](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/main/LICENSE.md). Before that it was under the Elastic License 2.0, which let you read the code but limited how you could use it.

Now you can use and change it for anything, including commercial work. The [TypeScript SDK](https://docs.paradev.com/sdk/getting-started) moved from Apache-2.0 to MIT too, so all of Paradev's own code has one license.

## Run it on your own cloud

You can run your agents on a Linux server in your own cloud account. Your repos and agent sign-ins are stored on that server. Agents call their model providers with your own keys, and when you open the server from another device, its terminal traffic goes through our relay. See the [Bring Your Own Cloud guide](https://docs.paradev.com/bring-your-own-cloud).

Or let [our cloud](https://docs.paradev.com/cloud-workspaces) keep them going while your laptop is closed. Either way, you can check on them from your phone.

## What stays the same

Nothing changes for your account or your plan. Your agents still run on your own subscriptions and keys.

## Contribute

The code is at [github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev), and [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) has the setup. Pull requests are welcome. If you build something on Paradev, please tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/cZeD9WYcV7).
