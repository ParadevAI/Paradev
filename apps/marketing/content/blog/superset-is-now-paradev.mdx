Today Superset becomes **Paradev**, short for parallel development, at [paradev.com](https://paradev.com). We also raised an $11.5M seed round, led by USV with Y Combinator and Paul Graham.

Only the name changes. Same app, same account, same team. There's nothing you need to do.

## Why a new name

Many companies and projects are called Superset, including an unrelated open-source project. We wanted a name that's truly ours, to build a lasting company on, and one that says what our users do every day: run coding agents in parallel.

## Our $11.5M seed

USV led the round, with Y Combinator and Paul Graham.

In under a year, more than 145,000 developers in 180 countries have created over 1.85 million agent workspaces, and one in eight Fortune 500 companies have used it. Today one engineer runs dozens of agents in parallel. The funding lets us build what comes next, a software factory for every engineering team:

- **Start.** Agents pick up work on a schedule or an event, not only when you type.
- **Run in parallel.** Each one works in its own workspace, with its own Git worktree.
- **Ready to merge.** You come back to reviewed changes, not a queue of half-done branches.

Our most advanced users already work this way. We want every team to.

## Join us

We're a small team, and we're hiring engineers who want to make hundreds of agents run reliably on one machine. See the open roles at [paradev.com/careers](https://paradev.com/careers), or write to [founders@paradev.com](mailto:founders@paradev.com).

## What changes

- **The name.** You'll see Paradev in the desktop app, the iPhone app, the docs and our emails.
- **The website.** [paradev.com](https://paradev.com), with docs at [docs.paradev.com](https://docs.paradev.com). Every superset.sh link redirects to the same page on paradev.com.
- **Our accounts.** X [@paradevai](https://x.com/paradevai), LinkedIn [/company/paradevai](https://www.linkedin.com/company/paradevai), YouTube [@paradevai](https://www.youtube.com/@paradevai) and GitHub [paradevai](https://github.com/paradevai). Old links redirect. Same [Discord](https://discord.gg/cZeD9WYcV7) server.
- **Emails.** For a few more weeks, emails still come from superset.sh, with the sender name "Paradev (formerly Superset)". Then they move to paradev.com.
- **Card statements.** Charges show as PARADEV.

## What doesn't change

- The product, its features and the team.
- Your account, workspaces, settings, agents, automations and data. You stay signed in.
- Pricing and billing: same plans, same prices.
- Your integrations, such as GitHub, Slack and Linear. A sign-in or consent screen may show the new name.
- The `superset` command in your terminal and the Homebrew tap.

## Thank you

Same people, new name, and the same goal: let every engineer run as many agents as their work needs. Thank you to everyone who has built with us, filed issues, and told a friend.
