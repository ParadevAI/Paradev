## Why

Sharing a name with other companies and projects has caused confusion. We want a name that's fully ours, so we're changing it while we're still early.

## What changes

- Our name and logo.
- Our website and email move to a new domain.

## What doesn't change

- The product, features and team.
- Your account, workspaces, settings, agents and billing.
- Your links and integrations. superset.sh points to the new domain, and existing docs links keep working.

The desktop app picks up the new name on its own, and the `superset` command in your terminal keeps working.

Follow along on [X](https://x.com/superset_sh), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/superset-sh) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/cZeD9WYcV7) for the reveal and some other news.

Thanks for building with us :)
