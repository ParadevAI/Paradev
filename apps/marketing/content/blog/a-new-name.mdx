## Why

There are many companies and projects with the same name. We've been looking for something that's truly ours to build an enduring company on. We've finally found it.

## What changes

We will make the change later this month. Only the name changes. Our logo, website and email domain change with it.

## What doesn't change

Nothing else will change: same product, team, and mission.

- The product, features and team.
- Your account, workspaces, settings, agents and billing.
- Your links and integrations. superset.sh points to the new domain, and existing docs links keep working.

The desktop app picks up the new name on its own, and the `superset` command in your terminal keeps working.

We'll continue to rewrite how software is written. Follow along on [X](https://x.com/superset_sh), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/superset-sh) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/cZeD9WYcV7) for the reveal and some other news.

Thanks for building with us :)
