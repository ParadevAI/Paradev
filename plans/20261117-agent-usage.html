What it says

  • Claude is the product for most people. 640 of 666 used it, and it has about 80% of chat sessions in every week since tracking began.
  • Codex is a real second, at 110 people. It doubled in the week of 8 November, from about 100 sessions to 214.
  • Most people use one agent. 535 used one, 118 used two, 12 used three or more. Nearly everyone who uses Codex also uses Claude.
  • OpenCode is the widest of the rest, with 17 people, mostly through the terminal.
  • Custom agents are the heaviest small group: 17 people, about 210 sessions. Their definitions live on the user's machine, so we cannot see what they run.
  • Oh My Pi and Pi have five people each over 30 days and use it a lot: 62 and 35 sessions. In the last 7 days Pi had one person and Oh My Pi none.
  • Everything else is one to three people in a month, and mostly nobody in a given week. Gemini, Grok, Antigravity, Cursor Agent and Mistral Vibe had no users in the last 7 days.
  • The weekly picture is narrower than the monthly one. Only four agents had more than one user in the last 7 days: Claude 269, Codex 48, custom agents 10, OpenCode 8.
  • Cloud is small but moving. 163 cloud sessions in the window, 129 of them in the week of 8 November. Cloud boxes only offer Claude and Codex, so cloud cannot show demand for anything else.

How far to trust it

  • Terminal use is under-counted. Terminal launches total 1,702 in the window against roughly 74,000 terminal prompts submitted. Agents that people mostly run in a terminal, such as OpenCode and Copilot, are probably more used than this shows.
  • Most workspace creations carry no agent. workspace_created fired 547,000 times in 30 days and records no agent; only the 2,859 workspace_create_requested events do. What creates the rest has not been traced.
  • Internal accounts are mostly still in. Excluding superset.sh and paradev.com emails removed only three people, which suggests most team accounts are not identified by those addresses. The cloud numbers in particular include our own use.
  • Chat sessions are new. The step up in the week of 20 September looks like a release, not organic growth, so week-on-week change before that says little.

Events used: new_session_started (chat sessions, with agent and local or cloud), workspace_create_requested (workspaces created with an agent), chat_message_sent, and agent_session_launch (terminal launches).
